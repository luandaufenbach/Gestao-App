Checkbox with a springy cobalt fill — `shape="round"` completes tasks, square is for settings and multi-select.

```jsx
<Checkbox shape="round" ariaLabel="Concluir tarefa" />
<Checkbox shape="round" strike defaultChecked label="Hero e seções da home" />
<Checkbox label="Lembretes de tarefas" description="Antes de cada prazo" />
```
